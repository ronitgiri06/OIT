About Us

A small institute with a stubborn habit: nobody leaves confused.

Om Institute Of Technology Pvt. Ltd. — ओम इंस्टीट्यूट ऑफ टेक्नोलॉजी पीवीटी. एलटीडी. — has been coaching computer science students from its Laxmi Nagar campus for years. Most of our students arrive through a friend's recommendation, and the reviews say why: the faculty is highly qualified, the teaching is clear, and help does not stop when the class does.

How we teach

Each topic starts on the board with the reasoning behind it, then moves to the machine where students write the code themselves. Assignments are checked individually, and unit tests before university exams show exactly where the gaps are. Mentors like Alok Sir are known for being patient, well-structured and approachable — the three things students mention most in their reviews.

Who studies here

BCA and MCA students from IP University and nearby colleges, B.Tech students catching up on core subjects, and complete beginners who want programming explained from zero. Final-year students also come for project guidance, documentation help and viva preparation.
